# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to this project, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive during code reviews and discussions.
- Show empathy toward other contributors and maintainers.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Offensive comments, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.
- Disruptive behavior or publishing private information without consent.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the project maintainer:
